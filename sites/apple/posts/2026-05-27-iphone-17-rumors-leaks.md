# iPhone 17: Everything We Know So Far About Apple's 2026 Lineup

## Introduction

Apple's iPhone 17 lineup is shaping up to be one of the most significant iPhone upgrades in years. With the September 2026 announcement approaching, leaks and rumors have painted a detailed picture of what to expect. From a completely redesigned chassis to Apple's first custom 5G modem chip, the iPhone 17 series represents Apple's vision for the next generation of smartphones.

The iPhone 17 lineup is expected to include four models: iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, and a new ultra-thin "iPhone 17 Air" that replaces the Plus model. This article compiles everything we know from credible leaks, supply chain reports, and analyst predictions.

## Section 1: Design and Display

### A New Design Language

The iPhone 17 series introduces the first major design overhaul since the iPhone 12 introduced flat edges in 2020. Key design changes include:

**Thinner Bezels**: Apple has achieved the thinnest bezels ever on a smartphone, with uniform 1.2mm bezels across all four sides of the display. This is achieved through a new Border Reduction Structure (BRS) technology.

**Titanium and Aluminum**: The Pro models continue with titanium frames, but with a new grade that is 15% lighter. The standard iPhone 17 and iPhone 17 Air use aerospace-grade aluminum.

**New Camera Module**: The camera bump has been redesigned into a horizontal "pill" shape that spans the width of the upper back, replacing the square module used since iPhone 11. This gives the iPhone 17 a distinctive, recognizable look.

**Colors**: iPhone 17 is expected in Black, White, Blue, Green, and Pink. The Pro models come in Space Black, White, Desert Titanium, and a new "Midnight Blue."

### Display Technology

All iPhone 17 models feature LTPO OLED displays with ProMotion 120Hz adaptive refresh rates — a first for the base iPhone model.

**Under-Display Face ID**: The Pro models move Face ID sensors under the display, eliminating the Dynamic Island in favor of a small circular camera cutout. The standard models retain the Dynamic Island.

## Section 2: Performance and Connectivity

### A19 and A19 Pro Chips

The iPhone 17 series is powered by Apple's A19 family of chips, manufactured on TSMC's 2nm process node.

**A19 (iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Air)**:
- 6-core CPU (2 performance + 4 efficiency)
- 5-core GPU
- 16-core Neural Engine
- 8GB RAM
- 20% faster CPU, 30% faster GPU vs. A18

**A19 Pro (iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max)**:
- 6-core CPU (2 performance + 4 efficiency)
- 6-core GPU
- 16-core Neural Engine
- 12GB RAM
- 25% faster CPU, 35% faster GPU vs. A18 Pro

The 2nm process delivers significant power efficiency improvements, contributing to longer battery life despite the more powerful hardware.

### Apple's Custom 5G Modem

The iPhone 17 series marks Apple's transition from Qualcomm modems to its own custom-designed 5G modem chip, internally codenamed "Sinope."

**Key Specifications**:
- Sub-6GHz and mmWave 5G support
- Download speeds up to 10 Gbps (theoretical peak)
- Improved power efficiency (30% less power than Qualcomm X75)
- Satellite connectivity improvements (Emergency SOS, Roadside Assistance)
- Wi-Fi 7 support with Apple's custom Wi-Fi chip

**Why This Matters**: Apple's custom modem gives the company end-to-end control over its wireless stack, enabling deeper integration with iOS, better power management, and independence from Qualcomm's licensing fees.

## FAQ

**Q1: Is the iPhone 17 a major upgrade over the iPhone 16?**

Yes, the iPhone 17 represents one of the biggest generational upgrades in recent years. The new design, under-display Face ID (Pro models), Apple's custom 5G modem, 2nm chips, and expanded Apple Intelligence features collectively make this a more significant upgrade than the iPhone 15 to iPhone 16 transition.

**Q2: What is the iPhone 17 Air?**

The iPhone 17 Air replaces the Plus model in Apple's lineup. It features a 6.6-inch display in an ultra-thin chassis (5.5mm thick), making it the thinnest iPhone ever. It targets users who want a larger screen without the bulk of the Pro Max.

**Q3: Will iPhone 17 have a portless design?**

No, the iPhone 17 series retains the USB-C port. While Apple has explored portless designs, the USB-C port is required for regulatory compliance in the EU and remains useful for accessories and data transfer.

**Q4: How does Apple's custom 5G modem compare to Qualcomm?**

Apple's Sinope modem delivers comparable performance to Qualcomm's latest modem with approximately 30% better power efficiency. The key advantage is deeper integration with iOS and Apple's silicon ecosystem, rather than raw speed differences.

**Q5: Should I upgrade to iPhone 17 or wait for iPhone 18?**

If you have an iPhone 14 or older, the iPhone 17 is a significant upgrade worth considering. If you have an iPhone 15 or 16, the improvements may not justify the cost unless you specifically want the new design, Apple Intelligence features, or the Air model's form factor.
